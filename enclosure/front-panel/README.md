# Alarm Clock Front Panel

This folder contains the front panel for the 3D-printed enclosure of the Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W alarm clock.

## Design

The front panel was designed in Autodesk Fusion 360 and printed in orange PLA+.

### Main dimensions

| Part | Dimensions |
|---|---:|
| Front panel | 120 × 55 × 3 mm |
| LCD opening | 72 × 25 mm |
| Sound holes | 7 × Ø2 mm |
| Sound-hole pattern radius | 4 mm |

The LCD opening is positioned 10 mm to the right of the panel centre.

## Features

- Opening for a 1602 LCD module
- Seven-hole sound grille for the buzzer
- Four rear mounting posts for the LCD
- Rear C-shaped clip for the buzzer
- Designed as part of a modular three-piece enclosure

## Material

The prototype was printed using orange PLA+ 2.0.

## Planned improvements

In a future iteration, three openings for tactile buttons will be added to the right side of the front panel. Matching 3D-printed button caps will also be designed and tested.

The up and down buttons will adjust the selected value, while the middle button will switch between settings such as time hours, time minutes, time seconds and alarm settings.

## Project status

The front panel has been test printed and the LCD and buzzer mounting solutions have been physically tested.
